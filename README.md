## 🎮 Controls

### 📱 Touch (Mobile & Tablet)
- **Virtual Joystick (Left Thumb):** 360° Movement and directional aim.
- **⚔️ SLASH:** Sword combo attacks.
- **🔫 FIRE:** Tap or hold to shoot your equipped firearm.
- **⚡ DASH:** Surge forward with invulnerability frames.
- **🌀 SPIN:** Area-of-effect spin-breaker move.
- **🔄 GUN:** Cycle through collected guns in your arsenal.
- **⛶ FULLSCREEN:** Expand to fill the mobile display.
- **⏸️ PAUSE:** Pause and resume game.

### 💻 Keyboard & Mouse (Desktop)
- **Movement:** <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> or Arrow Keys
- **Sword Attack:** <kbd>Enter</kbd> (3-hit combo)
- **Aim:** Mouse cursor (auto-locks to nearest threat when close)
- **Fire Gun:** <kbd>Shift</kbd>
- **Switch Weapons:** Left Mouse Click
- **Dash:** <kbd>Space</kbd>
- **Spin-Breaker:** <kbd>E</kbd>
- **Pause:** <kbd>P</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd>

## 🔫 Weapons & Power-ups
- **Pistol:** Reliable starter sidearm.
- **Shotgun:** Close-range 5-pellet spread.
- **Rifle:** Long-range high-damage penetrator.
- **Machine Gun:** Rapid-fire suppression.
- **Loot Drops:** Medkits (+HP), Fury (fast slashes), Might (+65% DMG), Guard (damage reduction), Haste (+speed), and Ammo crates.
